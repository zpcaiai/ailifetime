# External evidence gates

The adapters and verifier are production code, but this checkout has no authority to issue merchant, tax, legal, security, store or change-management decisions. Each row remains blocked until a real external artifact is bound to its SHA-256 digest and accepted through the signed production evidence path.

## B127 rule

`B127` may become `PASSED` only when the production verifier reads the live tenant through its TLS-verified, SELECT-only PostgreSQL 17/schema-4 role and finds both the prerequisite B126 certificate and the final `global`/100% B127 record. The signed manifest must bind the `full-commercial` profile, unique B00-B127 report hashes for all 640 requirements, every API/SDK/web/mobile/mini-program/config artifact version, all prior-wave observation reports, declared exceptions, the go-live window, operations handoff, SBOM, commit and every required external approval. B126 and B127 each require five distinct trusted, unexpired, unrevoked and correctly consumed Ed25519 envelopes, and may not reuse single-use evidence. Missing evidence is `BLOCKED_EXTERNAL`; it is never converted to success by configuration flags or client-authored booleans.
