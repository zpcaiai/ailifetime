## B127 rule

`B127` may become `PASSED` only when the production verifier reads the live tenant through its TLS-verified, SELECT-only PostgreSQL 17/schema-7 role and finds both the prerequisite B126 certificate and the final `global`/100% B127 record. Every schema-7 tenant table must have forced RLS and the exact single `tenant_isolation` policy (tenant-scoped core tables and partner-scoped partner projections); the verifier reads `pg_class` and `pg_policies` rather than trusting a report flag. The signed manifest must bind the `full-commercial` profile, unique B00-B127 report hashes for all 640 requirements, every API/SDK/web/mobile/mini-program/config artifact version, the exact consent/media/guardrail policy digests, the immutable authenticated media-authority projection bytes, declared exceptions, the go-live window, operations handoff, SBOM, commit and every required external approval. Each completed wave carries the closed eleven-domain observation plus digest-verified raw query/result and automatic-pause command/receipt artifacts, all bound to its release and time window. Every raw reference includes a digest-bound detached `ltm.control-artifact-attestation.v1` document: exact production Ed25519 authority-role enrollment, canonical claim-derived scope, release/deployment/wave/window/value/state binding, bounded validity, signature verification and one-time nonce consumption are mandatory. Query/result and pause-command/receipt producer keys must be independent; raw IDs/digests and attestation identities/signatures/digests cannot be reused across waves. The closed rollback-forward drill covers application, configuration, model, workflow, database, mobile, WeChat mini-program and vendor routing. The release subject and production-change approval bind the guardrail policy, aggregate wave set and rollback drill. Production change additionally requires distinct independently enrolled risk and operations Ed25519 approvers, both independent from the executor and primary signer; IDs or separation booleans are not evidence. A separate signed `runtime-policy-authority` deployment report verifies authenticated production policy probes. Missing evidence is `BLOCKED_EXTERNAL`; it is never converted to success by configuration flags or client-authored booleans.
