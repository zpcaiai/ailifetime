
# Incident runbook

## Incident

- Type: privacy | security | payment | partner | provider | quality | content abuse | availability | deletion | future-self
- Severity:
- Detected at:
- Incident commander:
- Legal/privacy/safety contacts:
- Affected regions/tenants/features:
- Current user impact:

## Immediate actions

- Confirm facts and preserve minimal evidence.
- Stop unsafe processing with the narrowest kill switch.
- Revoke exposed credentials/links if applicable.
- Protect payments/orders from duplicate actions.
- Suspend affected provider/model/template/partner.
- Keep consent withdrawal and privacy requests operable where possible.
- Record every privileged action.

## Investigation

Timeline, traces, workflow IDs, deployment changes, provider status, tenant scope, data/media exposure, payment/ledger, labels, and deletion state.

## Communication

Internal, partner, user, regulator/law-enforcement decision with qualified counsel and factual timestamps.

## Recovery

Validation commands, data reconciliation, restore/deletion checks, canary, and exit criteria.

## Post-incident

Root cause, contributing factors, control gaps, tests, owner, deadline, evidence, and risk acceptance.
