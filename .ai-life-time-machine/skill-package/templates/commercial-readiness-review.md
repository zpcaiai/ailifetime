# Commercial readiness review

## Scope
- Project profile and stack profile:
- Candidate commit and artifact manifest:
- Regions, channels, tenants, vendors, and launch waves:

## Required decisions
- [ ] Product, UX, accessibility, localization
- [ ] AI quality, safety, provenance, and cost
- [ ] Consent, privacy, child protection, deletion, and legal review
- [ ] Payments, ledger, tax/invoice, refunds, disputes, partner payouts
- [ ] Physical fulfillment, vendor contracts, logistics, returns, supplier deletion
- [ ] Support, escalation, customer success, status communications
- [ ] Security, supply chain, DR, capacity, observability, rollback
- [ ] Marketing claims, policy pages, app/mini-program review, partner enablement

## Exceptions
Every exception needs owner, risk, compensating control, expiry, evidence, and approval. Critical consent, safety, financial-integrity, tenant-isolation, deletion, or rollback gates cannot be waived.
