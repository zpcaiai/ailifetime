# Month-end close checklist
- [ ] Freeze source cut-off and verify payment-provider coverage
- [ ] Reconcile orders, captures, refunds, disputes and payout statements
- [ ] Balance journal entries and investigate suspense accounts
- [ ] Recognize revenue, update deferred/gift/credit liabilities and vendor accruals
- [ ] Validate invoices/fapiao, taxes, commissions and withholding records
- [ ] Reconcile AI, cloud, notification and fulfillment costs to dimensions
- [ ] Obtain preparer/reviewer sign-off and lock the period with an audit export
