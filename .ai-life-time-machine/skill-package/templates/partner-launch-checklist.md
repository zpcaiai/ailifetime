
# Partner launch checklist

- [ ] Legal entity and authorized contacts verified.
- [ ] Data roles, purposes, regions, retention, subprocessors, deletion, incident, and termination are documented.
- [ ] Contract terms are translated into a versioned machine policy.
- [ ] Allowed products/channels are explicit.
- [ ] Face, child, public-figure, deceased-person, future-self, and AI-label rules are accepted.
- [ ] Insurance/retirement partner has signed the no-underwriting/no-pricing/no-claims/no-sensitive-lead boundary.
- [ ] Sandbox keys, quotas, test fixtures, webhooks, and SSO are validated.
- [ ] Cross-tenant and permission tests pass.
- [ ] Customer consent flow is hosted/verified; partner cannot self-assert consent.
- [ ] Brand assets pass security, rights, accessibility, and mandatory-disclosure checks.
- [ ] Payment, refund, support, SLA, and escalation paths pass.
- [ ] Usage and settlement reconciliation passes with duplicates, refunds, and late events.
- [ ] Security/privacy review and incident contacts pass.
- [ ] Data-rights and partner termination exercise passes.
- [ ] Pilot metrics, stop conditions, rollback, and owner approvals are recorded.
- [ ] Production feature flag remains off until launch certificate is signed.
